# Domain 3.3: Cyber Security Operations and Technology

Domain 3.3 is the largest: people, premises, assets, architecture, access, applications, change, infrastructure, cryptography, personal devices, disposal, payment systems, electronic banking, and the four operational subdomains - event management, incident management, threat management and vulnerability management.

Most subdomains here follow the same pattern: a standard or process that is defined, approved and implemented; compliance with it monitored; its effectiveness measured; and then what it must contain.

Banks must reach maturity level 4 in 3.3.14 to 3.3.17.

Part of [the SAMA handbook](sama-01-how-assessment-works.md). Previous: [Domain 3.2](sama-03-risk-compliance.md). Next: [Domain 3.4](sama-05-third-party.md).

The domain, subdomain and control numbers, and the subdomain names, are SAMA's. Control titles and all the wording below are ours; read SAMA's own text for the same number on rulebook.sama.gov.sa.

## 3.3.1 Human Resources

**What SAMA requires.** Cyber security is built into human resources processes: staff agreements carry security responsibilities, and staff are screened before and during employment.

### 3.3.1.1 Security requirements in HR processes

**What it asks.** The human resources process defines, approves and implements cyber security requirements.

**What to do.** Write the security steps into HR procedures and have them approved.

**Level 3 looks like.** Defined and approved in writing, carried out in a structured way, shown with evidence, and checked for compliance.

**Records to keep.** Approved HR procedures containing the security requirements.

### 3.3.1.2 HR security effectiveness monitored and evaluated

**What it asks.** The effectiveness of the human resources process is monitored, measured and periodically evaluated.

**What to do.** Track measures such as screening completed before start dates and access removed on leaving dates, and review them.

**Level 3 looks like.** Defined and approved in writing, carried out in a structured way, shown with evidence, and checked for compliance.

**Records to keep.** HR security measures; evaluation records.

*Measuring and periodically evaluating effectiveness is how SAMA's maturity model describes level 4. It is still one of this subdomain's control considerations.*

### 3.3.1.3 What the HR process includes

**What it asks.** The HR process includes: security responsibilities and non-disclosure clauses in staff agreements, during and after employment; security awareness at the start of employment and during it; when disciplinary action applies; screening and background checks; and security steps when employment ends, such as revoking access and returning assets (badges, tokens, mobile devices and all electronic and physical information).

**What to do.** Check the HR process covers each item, and keep records per person.

**Level 3 looks like.** Defined and approved in writing, carried out in a structured way, shown with evidence, and checked for compliance.

**Records to keep.** Staff agreements with security and non-disclosure clauses; screening records; induction awareness records; leaver checklists showing access revoked and assets returned.

*Measuring and periodically evaluating effectiveness is how SAMA's maturity model describes level 4. It is still one of this subdomain's control considerations.*

### 3.3.5.4 What the identity and access management policy includes

**What it asks.** The policy includes: business requirements for access, on need-to-have and need-to-know; user access management for joiners, movers and leavers, covering every type of user, with staff changes started by HR and third-party changes started by the accountable party, requests formally approved, changes processed promptly, access reviewed periodically, and an audit trail of requests, approvals and revocations; automation to support access management; a centralised identity and access function; multi-factor authentication for sensitive and critical systems and profiles; and management of privileged and remote access, with multi-factor authentication for all remote access and, based on risk assessment, for privileged access to critical systems, periodic review of privileged and remote users, individual accountability, and controls on shared privileged accounts (limited and monitored, passwords kept confidential and changed often and at the end of each session).

**What to do.** Check the policy against each item. Evidence the key ones from the systems: MFA settings, access review sign-offs, and leaver removals matched to HR dates.

**Level 3 looks like.** Defined and approved in writing, carried out in a structured way, shown with evidence, and checked for compliance.

**Records to keep.** Access request and approval records; access review sign-offs; MFA coverage; privileged account list and reviews; shared account password records.

### 3.3.13.4 What the electronic banking standard covers

**What it asks.** The standard covers four areas. Brand protection for online services, including social media.
