# What each screen is for

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Get ready** | The plan: what to do next, and how far you have got |
| **Golden thread** | Every risk, what treats it, and the proof, joined up. Broken links are what auditors find |
| **Dashboard** | Where you stand today: maturity by domain, and the distance to your targets |
| **Controls** | All 136 controls in SAMA's 32 subdomains: maturity level, target, owner, evidence, notes |
| **Evidence** | The proof, with dates and which controls each item supports |
| **Risks** | What could go wrong, scored, owned and treated |
| **Assets** | What you hold that matters, and who owns it |
| **Policies** | Your scope, and your documents with their versions and review dates |
| **Tasks** | The work outstanding, with owners and dates |
| **Incidents** | What went wrong, how bad, and where it stands |
| **Findings** | What audits and reviews found, and what closed it |
| **Reports** | PDFs for the board or SAMA: executive summary, maturity assessment, gap report and more |
| **Suppliers** | Third parties, outsourcing and cloud providers, and the assurance they gave (3.4). Has a sample library |
| **Training** | Who was trained, on what, and when it is due again (3.1.6 awareness, 3.1.7 training) |
| **Objectives** | What the cyber security strategy is aiming at, and whether you are getting there (3.1.2) |
| **Interested parties** | Who has a stake in your security, SAMA included, and what they need |
| **Audits & reviews** | Cyber security reviews and audits, planned and held (3.2.4, 3.2.5) |
| **Communications** | What you tell people about security, to whom, how often, and when it is next due |
| **Escalation** | Automatic reminders for anything with a date and an owner's email, one email each, and who is told as it goes overdue |
| **Calendar** | Everything with a date on it, in one list |
| **Help** | Short answers, like this page |
| **Documentation** | The longer guides |
| **Users** | Who can sign in, and what they are allowed to do |
| **Audit trail** | Who did what, when, and from where. Read only. Administrators and auditors |
| **Backups** | Take one, download one, restore one |
| **Settings** | Email, the daily digest, updates, and the HTTPS certificate |

## Two things that surprise people

**Level 3 is the pass mark, not level 5.** Every control starts with a target of
3, which is what SAMA expects. Raise a target only where the business needs
more. Chasing 5 everywhere wastes the budget that should close your real gaps.

**A score is a claim until proof is attached.** A level 3 with nothing in
Evidence behind it is the first thing an assessor will question.
