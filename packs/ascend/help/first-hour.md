# Your first hour

Six things, in this order. None of them takes long, and everything else works
better once they are done.

## 1. Add the people who will do the work

**Users → Add someone.** Give each person the smallest role that lets them do
their job.

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Administrator | Everything, plus adding people and changing settings |
| Contributor | Read and change all compliance data. Most people |
| Auditor | Read everything, including the audit trail. Change nothing |
| Read only | Read everything. Change nothing |

An Auditor account is what you give your internal audit team, or whoever
assesses you, so they can look at the scores and the proof themselves.

## 2. Turn on email

**Settings → Outgoing email.** Put in your mail server and send yourself a test
message.

Without it nobody is reminded of anything, and an administrator who forgets
their password cannot get back in on their own.

## 3. Put your logo on reports

**Reports → your logo.** Every PDF you hand to the board or to SAMA carries it.
One minute.

## 4. Check the backup

**Backups.** One is taken every night. Press **Download** on the newest one and
keep that copy somewhere other than this server.

A backup that has never left the machine protects you from very little.

## 5. Write your scope

**Policies.** The scope is the box at the top. A paragraph: which company,
which services, which locations, which systems, and what is deliberately left
out.

Every score you give later is a score for what is inside that box.

## 6. Open Get ready

**Get ready** is the plan: seven stages, 24 steps, from setting the product up
to closing your gaps. It tells you what to do, why it matters, and takes you to
the screen where you do it. Steps the product can check itself go green on
their own.

Start at stage 1 and work down. You do not have to follow the order.
