# Using it day to day

Getting to level 3 once is not the hard part. Staying there is.

## Every day: the digest

Turn on the **daily digest** in Settings. One email lists what needs
attention: evidence out of date, overdue tasks and findings, policies due for
review, and the risks in the top band. If it is empty, nothing is slipping.

## Once a week: the tasks

Open **Tasks**. Close what is done, and change the date on anything that has
slipped, with a reason. A pile of overdue tasks nobody has looked at is the
clearest sign that the programme has stopped.

## When you fix something: re-score it

A task closed, a tool bought, a process written down? Open the control, update
its level, and attach the new proof. Two minutes then, or an afternoon of
remembering later.

## When something goes wrong: the incident

**Incidents.** What happened, how bad, where it stands. If it is classified medium
or high, control 3.3.15.5 asks you to tell SAMA IT Risk Supervision immediately,
and 3.3.15.7 asks for a formal report once operations resume. Record when you
did both, and note the root cause and what you changed afterwards (3.3.15.4).

## When something is found: the finding

**Findings.** An internal audit, an external audit or SAMA itself found
something. Record what, the root cause, what you changed, and how you checked
it worked. Control 3.2.4.5 asks for exactly this: follow-up until every issue is addressed.

## Every quarter: the evidence and the risks

Evidence older than 90 days is flagged. Collect it again, or change what you
are claiming. Look at the top of the risk register and ask whether it still
reflects reality.

## Every quarter: the maturity assessment

Export the **Maturity assessment** from **Reports** and keep it. Laid side by
side, four quarters of them show the board what its money bought.

## Once a year: the self-assessment

SAMA assesses the framework through a periodic self-assessment, which SAMA
then reviews and audits (section 2.3 of the framework). Walk every control,
confirm or correct its level, refresh the proof, and export the
**Executive summary** and the **Maturity assessment** as the record of it.
