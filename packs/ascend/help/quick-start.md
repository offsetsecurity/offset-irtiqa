# Quick start

Fifteen minutes. By the end you will know what the product holds, and what to
do tomorrow.

## Sign in

Your administrator gives you a username, a password and the address. The first
person ever to open a new install creates the administrator account.

## Look at Get ready

Seven stages, 24 steps, from setting the product up to closing your gaps. Each
step says what to do, why it matters, and has a button to the screen where you
do it.

Two kinds of step:

- **Checked for you.** The product looks at its own data and turns them green.
- **Ticked by you.** Things no software can see, such as whether the board
  approved a policy.

## Write the scope

**Policies**, the box at the top. A paragraph on what this programme covers is
enough to start.

## Put something in two registers

- **Assets:** open the **Sample library** tab and add the ten that look like
  yours. There are 98, including ones specific to the Saudi financial sector.
- **Risks:** the same. There are 43 to react to, written for financial
  services.

Reacting to a list beats staring at a blank page, which is where most first
attempts stop.

## Score ten controls

Open **Controls** and pick ten you know well. Read the guidance on each: what
it is for, what level 3 looks like, and what an assessor looks for. Then set
the level that is true today, from 0 to 5.

Ten honest scores today beat 136 hopeful ones next month.

## Attach one piece of proof

**Evidence → Add.** Attach a file, date it, and link it to a control. That is
the loop you will repeat for the rest of the programme: score, fix, prove.

## Tomorrow

Export the **Gap report** from **Reports**. It lists every control below its
target. The top of that list is your first week's work.
