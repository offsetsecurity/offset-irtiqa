# SAMA document set (01 to 34)

> Starting points, not finished documents. Open **Policies** → **Templates**, fill them in, have them approved, then record each one in **Policies** with its owner and review date.

SAMA's level 3 is "defined and approved, implemented, and compliance monitored". A control that is done well but not written down stays at level 2. This set gives you a document for each area the framework calls for. Every document ends with the SAMA controls it answers, how compliance is monitored, and the indicators used to measure effectiveness.

All of it is our own writing, using SAMA's control numbers. Read the framework itself at rulebook.sama.gov.sa.

## Fill them in here

Press **Fill in** at the top of **Policies** → **Templates**.

1. **About your organisation** is asked once and used in every document: the name, the CISO, the committee chair and how to report an incident.
2. **Your choices** already have a usual answer. Change any that do not fit.
3. Each document then asks only what is its own.

Your risks, assets, incidents, suppliers, training records and audits go in from the screens where you already keep them. You are not asked for them again. Anything you leave empty stays yellow in the file. Download a document at any time, or **Download all filled (zip)** for the lot.

## Good to know

- SAMA approvals come first. Some things need SAMA's no-objection or approval before you act: a CISO, material outsourcing, cloud services, a new electronic banking service. Document 32 lists them.
- The committee approves the key documents and the board endorses the governance, strategy and policy. Each document says which.
- A document is a starting point. If a sentence does not describe what you really do, change it to match.
