# Common questions

**Do I have to do the stages in order?**
No. Get ready suggests an order most organisations find easiest. Do anything you
can already do.

**A step will not tick.**
Steps with "The product checks this one itself" go green from your data. The
line underneath says what is missing. Steps only a person can confirm are
ticked by hand.

**Something does not apply to us.**
Every step can be excluded with a reason, and so can every control. Excluded
things stop counting against you. Be strict: "we have no ATMs" is a reason,
"we have not got round to it" is not.

**What does level 3 actually need?**
All four: it is written down, it is approved, people actually follow it, and
somebody checks that they do. Missing any one of them is level 2, however good
the practice is.

**Can a control aim higher than 3?**
Yes. Open the control and raise its target level. The dashboard and the gap
report measure against the target you set.

**Should we score what we plan to do?**
No. Score what is true today. An optimistic score produces a gap report that
hides the work, and an assessor who finds one inflated score doubts the rest.

**How fresh does evidence have to be?**
The product flags anything older than 90 days. Set a collected date on
everything.

**We are a small team. Is this overkill?**
Use Get ready and the sample libraries, and mark what genuinely does not apply.
The framework scales; the paperwork should too.

**Does this make us compliant with SAMA?**
No. It is where you record and show your work. The judgement on your maturity
is made by SAMA, or by whoever assesses you on its behalf.

**Why does the browser say "Not secure"?**
The server has no certificate the browser trusts. An administrator fixes it
for everyone under **Settings → HTTPS certificate**, with a certificate from
your IT team. It is free: you do not need to buy one.
