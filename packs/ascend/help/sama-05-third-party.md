# Domain 3.4: Third Party Cyber Security

Domain 3.4 covers the organisations you rely on: contract and vendor management, outsourcing, and hybrid and public cloud. Two of its controls need SAMA's approval before you act: material outsourcing, and using a cloud service.

Part of [the SAMA handbook](sama-01-how-assessment-works.md). Previous: [Domain 3.3](sama-04-operations-technology.md). Next: [Documents, approvals and reports to SAMA](sama-06-documents-and-approvals.md).

The domain, subdomain and control numbers, and the subdomain names, are SAMA's. Control titles and all the wording below are ours; read SAMA's own text for the same number on rulebook.sama.gov.sa.

## 3.4.1 Contract and Vendor Management

**What SAMA requires.** Security controls are part of contract and vendor management, so security requirements are settled before a contract is signed and checked throughout its life.

### 3.4.1.1 Security requirements in contract and vendor management

**What it asks.** Security requirements are defined, approved, implemented and communicated within the contract and vendor management processes.

**What to do.** Write the security steps into procurement and vendor management procedures.

**Level 3 looks like.** Defined and approved in writing, carried out in a structured way, shown with evidence, and checked for compliance.

**Records to keep.** Procurement and vendor procedures containing the security requirements.

### 3.4.1.2 Compliance with the contract and vendor management process is monitored

**What it asks.** Compliance with the contract and vendor management process is monitored.

**What to do.** Decide how compliance is checked and how often, and record each check.

**Level 3 looks like.** Defined and approved in writing, carried out in a structured way, shown with evidence, and checked for compliance.

**Records to keep.** Compliance check results; exceptions and what was done about them.

### 3.4.1.3 Effectiveness of the contract and vendor security controls measured and evaluated

**What it asks.** The effectiveness of the contract and vendor security controls is measured and periodically evaluated.

**What to do.** Set measures that show whether the controls work, review them on a schedule, and record the improvements you make.

**Level 3 looks like.** Defined and approved in writing, carried out in a structured way, shown with evidence, and checked for compliance.

**Records to keep.** Effectiveness measures and trend reports; evaluation records; improvement actions.

*Measuring and periodically evaluating effectiveness is how SAMA's maturity model describes level 4. It is still one of this subdomain's control considerations.*

### 3.4.1.4 What contract and vendor management covers

**What it asks.** The processes cover: when the cyber security function must be involved, for example in due diligence; baseline security requirements that apply in every case; and the right to carry out periodic security reviews and audits.

**What to do.** Put these three into the procedures and the standard contract terms.

**Level 3 looks like.** Defined and approved in writing, carried out in a structured way, shown with evidence, and checked for compliance.

**Records to keep.** Procedures; standard contract clauses including audit rights.

### 3.4.1.5 What contract management requires

**What it asks.** Contract management requires: a security risk assessment during procurement; specific security requirements in the tender; evaluating vendors' answers to them; risk-based testing of the agreed requirements; an escalation process for security incidents; security requirements for exit, termination or renewal, including escrow where relevant; and a mutual confidentiality agreement.

**What to do.** Follow each step for every relevant contract, and keep the evidence on the file.

**Level 3 looks like.** Defined and approved in writing, carried out in a structured way, shown with evidence, and checked for compliance.

**Records to keep.** Procurement risk assessments; tender security requirements and evaluations; test results; incident escalation terms; exit terms; confidentiality agreements.

### 3.4.1.6 Vendor performance on security reviewed

**What it asks.** Vendor management, through service level management, covers periodic reporting, review and evaluation of the security requirements agreed in the contract.

**What to do.** Include security measures in SLAs and review them with each vendor.

**Level 3 looks like.** Defined and approved in writing, carried out in a structured way, shown with evidence, and checked for compliance.

**Records to keep.** SLA reports; vendor review minutes.

## 3.4.2 Outsourcing

**What SAMA requires.** Security controls are part of the outsourcing policy and process, so security requirements are met before, during and at the end of outsourcing.

### 3.4.2.1 Security requirements in outsourcing

**What it asks.** Security requirements in the outsourcing policy and process are defined, approved, implemented and communicated.

**What to do.** Write the security requirements into the outsourcing policy and process.

**Level 3 looks like.** Defined and approved in writing, carried out in a structured way, shown with evidence, and checked for compliance.

**Records to keep.** The outsourcing policy with its security requirements.

### 3.4.2.2 Outsourcing security requirements measured and evaluated

**What it asks.** The security requirements in the outsourcing policy and process are measured and periodically evaluated.

**What to do.** Review outsourced services against the requirements on a schedule.

**Level 3 looks like.** Defined and approved in writing, carried out in a structured way, shown with evidence, and checked for compliance.

**Records to keep.** Evaluation records for outsourced services.

*Measuring and periodically evaluating effectiveness is how SAMA's maturity model describes level 4. It is still one of this subdomain's control considerations.*

### 3.4.2.3 SAMA approval and the SAMA outsourcing circular

**What it asks.** The outsourcing process includes SAMA's approval before any material outsourcing, involvement of the cyber security function, and compliance with SAMA's circular on outsourcing.

**What to do.** Identify which outsourcing is material, obtain SAMA's approval first, and follow the current SAMA outsourcing rules.

**Level 3 looks like.** Defined and approved in writing, carried out in a structured way, shown with evidence, and checked for compliance.

**Records to keep.** SAMA approvals for material outsourcing; security function sign-off; compliance with the outsourcing rules.

## 3.4.3 Cloud Computing

**What SAMA requires.** Security controls are part of the cloud computing policy and process for hybrid and public cloud services. This does not apply to private (internal) cloud.

### 3.4.3.1 Cloud security controls: defined, approved, implemented and communicated

**What it asks.** The security controls in the cloud computing policy for hybrid and public cloud services are defined, approved, implemented and communicated.

**What to do.** Write the cloud policy, have it approved, and communicate it.

**Level 3 looks like.** Defined and approved in writing, carried out in a structured way, shown with evidence, and checked for compliance.

**Records to keep.** The approved cloud computing policy.

### 3.4.3.2 Compliance with the cloud computing policy is monitored

**What it asks.** Compliance with the cloud computing policy is monitored.

**What to do.** Decide how compliance is checked and how often, and record each check.

**Level 3 looks like.** Defined and approved in writing, carried out in a structured way, shown with evidence, and checked for compliance.

**Records to keep.** Compliance check results; exceptions and what was done about them.

### 3.4.3.3 Cloud security controls measured and evaluated

**What it asks.** The security controls in the cloud computing policy and process for hybrid and public cloud services are periodically measured and evaluated.

**What to do.** Review cloud controls against the policy on a schedule.

**Level 3 looks like.** Defined and approved in writing, carried out in a structured way, shown with evidence, and checked for compliance.

**Records to keep.** Evaluation records.

*Measuring and periodically evaluating effectiveness is how SAMA's maturity model describes level 4. It is still one of this subdomain's control considerations.*

### 3.4.3.4 What the cloud policy requires

**What it asks.** The policy requires: a risk assessment and due diligence on the provider and its services; SAMA's approval before using cloud services or signing with the provider; a contract with security requirements in place before use; data kept in Saudi Arabia in principle, with SAMA's explicit approval for any service outside it; no use of the organisation's data by the provider for other purposes; the provider implementing and monitoring the controls the risk assessment calls for; the organisation's data logically separated from other customers' and always identifiable; business continuity in line with the organisation's policy; the right to carry out security reviews, audits and examinations at the provider; and on exit, termination rights and the provider returning the data and then irreversibly deleting it.

**What to do.** Check each requirement for every hybrid or public cloud service before it is used.

**Level 3 looks like.** Defined and approved in writing, carried out in a structured way, shown with evidence, and checked for compliance.

**Records to keep.** Cloud risk assessments and due diligence; SAMA approvals; contracts with the required terms; data location evidence; exit and deletion terms.
