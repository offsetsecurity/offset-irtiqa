# SAMA Cyber Security Framework playbook

The framework asks every member organisation to reach **maturity level 3** on
every control, and to be able to show it. Banks must reach **level 4** in four
subdomains. This is the path, and where each piece lives here.

Everything below is also in **Get ready**, which tracks how far you have got.

## The maturity scale

| Level | Name | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Non-existent | No documentation, no awareness |
| 1 | Ad-hoc | Done inconsistently, not fully defined |
| 2 | Repeatable but informal | Repeated, but not formally defined or approved |
| 3 | Structured and formalised | Defined, approved, implemented, shown, and checked for compliance |
| 4 | Managed and measurable | Effectiveness is measured and periodically evaluated |
| 5 | Adaptive | Continuously improved, and compared with the sector |

To reach a level, every criterion of the levels below it must be met first. The
jump that matters is from 2 to 3. Most organisations already do the work; what
is missing is the writing down, the approval and the checking.

**Banks: level 4 for four subdomains.** SAMA's circular 29814/67 requires banks
to meet level 4 for all components of 3.3.14 Cyber Security Event Management,
3.3.15 Cyber Security Incident Management, 3.3.16 Threat Management and 3.3.17
Vulnerability Management. Set the target on those controls to 4.

## How the controls are numbered

Controls follow the framework's own numbering. Control 3.1.1.4 is control
consideration 4 in subdomain 3.1.1 Cyber Security Governance. You can open the
same number on rulebook.sama.gov.sa and read SAMA's text beside ours.

## Step 1: set up and scope

Add your people, turn on email, and write the scope in **Policies**. Record the
CISO as the owner of control 3.1.1.8. SAMA's no-objection is needed before the
CISO is appointed (3.1.1.9).

## Step 2: know what you have

**Assets**: processes, applications, infrastructure, data and the suppliers you
depend on, each with an owner (3.3.3.3). The Sample library has plenty to start
from.

## Step 3: score honestly

Work through **Controls**. Mark what genuinely does not apply, with a reason.
If you are not a bank, SAMA excludes 3.2.3, 3.3.12 and 3.3.13, with two
exceptions: PCI DSS or the SWIFT framework if you handle card data or use SWIFT,
and multi-factor authentication if you offer online services. Score everything
else as it is today.

## Step 4: write it down and get it approved

The governance, the strategy and the policy, approved by the committee and
endorsed by the board (3.1.1 to 3.1.4). Then the standards and procedures your
low scores point at. This is the cheapest way to move many controls from 2 to 3
at once.

## Step 5: manage the risk

**Risks**: scored, owned, with a decision on each, and linked to the controls
that reduce them. Risk owners accept the results (3.2.1.10), and the risk
appetite is approved (3.2.1.11). Without a risk register, most of 3.2.1 cannot
get past level 2.

## Step 6: prove it

**Evidence**: every control you scored 3 or above needs proof attached, linked
and dated. Start with the highest scores; they are the ones an assessor tests
first.

## Step 7: close the gaps and keep going

Export the **Gap report**, raise a task for each gap with an owner and a date,
and re-score when the task closes. Take the **Maturity assessment** to the
committee every quarter.

## What an assessor asks for, and where it lives

| Asked for | Controls | Where |
|---|---|---|
| The committee, its charter and minutes | 3.1.1.1 to 3.1.1.4 | Policies, Evidence |
| The CISO appointment and SAMA's no-objection | 3.1.1.8, 3.1.1.9 | Evidence |
| An approved strategy and policy | 3.1.2, 3.1.3 | Policies |
| Awareness and training records | 3.1.6, 3.1.7 | Training, Communications, Evidence |
| The risk method, risk appetite and risk register | 3.2.1 | Policies, Risks, and the risk register report |
| Reviews, penetration tests and audits, and their follow-up | 3.2.4, 3.2.5 | Audits & reviews, Findings, Tasks |
| The asset register, with owners and classification | 3.3.3.3 | Assets |
| Access reviews and multi-factor authentication | 3.3.5.4 | Evidence |
| Change records | 3.3.7.4 | Evidence |
| SOC, SIEM and 24x7 monitoring | 3.3.14 | Evidence |
| Incident handling, and reports to SAMA | 3.3.15 | Incidents, Findings |
| Vulnerability scans and patch timelines | 3.3.17.3 | Evidence |
| Third parties, outsourcing and cloud | 3.4.1 to 3.4.3 | Suppliers, Risks, Evidence |
| The overall maturity picture | all | Maturity assessment and Executive summary reports |

Business continuity is not in this framework. SAMA covers it in its separate
Business Continuity Minimum Requirements.
