# Offset Irtiqa

**For the SAMA Cyber Security Framework.** Score your maturity, close the gaps
to level 3, and show the proof. On your own server.

## Who it is for

Banks, insurers, finance companies and payment providers regulated by the
Saudi Central Bank, and the consultants who help them. Especially a team
without a large security function, because the product tells you what to do
next.

## What you get

| | |
|---|---|
| **The framework** | 136 controls in SAMA's 32 subdomains and 4 domains, numbered as SAMA numbers them |
| **Maturity scoring** | Each control scored 0 to 5, against a target of 3 that you can raise (banks: 4 for the four subdomains SAMA names) |
| **Guidance on every control** | What it is for, what level 3 looks like, and what an assessor looks for |
| **Get ready** | A plan of 7 stages and 24 steps, from setting up to closing gaps. 13 steps tick themselves from your data |
| **Sample library** | 98 assets and 43 risks written for financial services and the Saudi financial sector, to add with one click |
| **Registers** | Evidence, risks, assets, policies, tasks, incidents and findings, all linked to the controls |
| **Reports (PDF, with your logo)** | Executive summary, maturity assessment, gap report, readiness plan, risk register, evidence register |
| **Help and Documentation** | 10 pages in the product, including a SAMA playbook and where to find evidence in Microsoft 365, AWS and Google |

## Every Offset product also has

HTTPS with your certificate · four roles · every change recorded in an audit trail that auditors can read and download · nightly
backups and restore from the screen · signed one-click updates · email
reminders and a daily digest · Forgot password for administrators.

## How it installs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | One installer. Nothing else to install |
| **Linux** | A package that installs as a system service. Ubuntu, Debian, RHEL, Amazon Linux, SUSE |
| **Docker** | Pull the image and run it with Compose |
| **Needs** | A 64-bit server, about 300 MB of disk plus your evidence, and one port. It uses under 150 MB of memory at rest |
| **Sized for** | Up to about 50 users |

## Your data stays yours

It runs on your server, inside your network. No cloud, no telemetry, no
licence server. Offset Security has no access to it. Details are in the
security overview and the privacy sheet.
